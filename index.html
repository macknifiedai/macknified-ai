FAQ

Straight answers.

What is MCP and why does it matter?

Model Context Protocol is the open standard AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT use to call outside tools. Macknified AI supports it, so your assistant can build pages, add contacts, run automations and more inside your account — you just ask.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Type what you want in plain English, or use the no-code builders in the dashboard. Builders who do code can drop in their own HTML, CSS and functions.

What happens after the free trial?

Your Basic trial runs 7 days. After that it's $49/month (regularly $149/month). Seats at this price are limited. Cancel anytime.

What’s the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic starts with a 7-day free trial, then $49/month, and includes 3 AI agents. Pro is $79/month and includes everything in Basic, plus unlimited AI agents, Apple & Google Wallet passes, loyalty & e-commerce, the Content Studio, CRM up to 50,000 contacts and 5 team seats.

Can I use it for my clients?

Yes. Builders use it to create pages, agents and automations for the businesses they work with.
